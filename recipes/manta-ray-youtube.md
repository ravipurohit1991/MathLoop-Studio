## Title

Building a 3D Manta Ray with Fourier Series | Episode 1

## Description

From a single ring to a manta ray in 3D. Watch rotating circles trace individual contours, assemble the form, and reveal the finished creature.

This is the first episode of my 3D Fourier Creatures series, exploring how mathematics can describe form in three dimensions.

Each contour is reconstructed using a vector Fourier series. Neighbouring contours form the surface, and an authored travelling wave animates the fins.

Created with JavaScript: mathematical reconstruction, procedural geometry, and animation in a 60-second film.
