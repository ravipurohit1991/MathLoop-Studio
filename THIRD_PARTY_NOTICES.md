# Third-party assets

The photographic panoramas in `assets/nature/` are from **Poly Haven** and are
distributed under **CC0-1.0**. Their original photographers and processing
credits, source URLs, checksums, and resize information are retained in
[`assets/nature/sources.json`](assets/nature/sources.json).

- [Poly Haven license](https://polyhaven.com/license)
- [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

These photographs have separate provenance from the MIT-licensed application.
The mathematical creatures and procedural scores are defined in this project's
source. Whale Song uses synthesized calls, not a wildlife recording.

Dependencies retain their respective licenses; see the installed packages for
their notices. Operating-system fonts and speech engines used during rendering
are not bundled with this repository. The launch trailer uses a local Windows
synthetic voice and the application's Aurora Glass score.
